eraya is not a gallery. It is a collection of moments captured on canvas — each one painted slowly, by hand, with acrylic on stretched canvas and finished with fine art bristles that leave nothing to chance.


Every work is an original. There are no prints, no reproductions. When a piece finds its home, it is gone — and another story begins. These paintings are made for people who believe that certain objects are not merely decorative: they are necessary. They are requisite.

An Ode

In Hindu tradition, no new venture begins without first offering an ode to Lord Ganesha — remover of obstacles, keeper of new beginnings. This painting is exactly that.


Amber and gold on the deepest black, Ganesha meditates in serenity. At his side, a white lotus — symbol of purity, of fortune, and of the threshold between the world and the sanctuary of home.


This is eraya's first work — the painting that opened every door that followed.

Acrylic on Canvas · Framed · One of One

The Blues

Imagine a child growing up far from Italy — knowing the Colosseum only from photographs, Venice only from postcards. The Blues Collection is that child's reimagining: great Italian landmarks in deep indigo on pale linen. Not what these places are, but what they feel like when you have loved them from a distance your entire life.
